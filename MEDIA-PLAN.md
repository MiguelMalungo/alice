# Alice in Brewland — Media Plan

What to do with the three archives delivered on 14 Sep 2026 (`7. Signature Coffee.zip`, `0. SlideShow Movie (1).zip`, `FOTOS SITE ALICE.zip`), and how each piece improves the live site at https://miguelmalungo.github.io/alice/.

Sources are kept in `assets/client-media/`. Nothing below is built yet; this is the plan to approve.

---

## 0. The headline changes

1. **Fix the rabbit hole.** The five drink cut-outs in the tunnel were cut from ~1000 px PDF pages and look cropped and soft. Replace them with the six Signature Coffee product shots: 1254 px, studio-lit, clean edges, each sitting on its own wooden board, which reads as a real object floating past. Add the round "Coffee Lovers" badge as a slowly spinning coin.
2. **A new chapter: Signature Coffee.** The folder is numbered "7." with its own button and background art, so it is clearly meant to be a section. The site has nothing about the coffee itself beyond one card. This becomes the strongest food moment on the page.
3. **A magic lantern in "A Experiência".** The ten slideshow frames become a slow, crossfading, gently zooming picture inside a storybook frame, with a hand crank. On-brand for 1865, and the only place the polished campaign frames sit comfortably next to the real phone photos.
4. **Replace every low-resolution photo** with the high-res version now available, and use the new real photos (storefront, wall logo, barista, checkerboard room) where the page currently has nothing.
5. **A real menu.** The media names eleven dishes and drinks. The four flip cards grow into a proper, photographed menu, and the overlay gets a second page for the signature coffees.

## 2. Section by section

### Hero
No photos; the concept stays the rabbit, the wordmark and the hole. Two small additions: the round badge becomes the favicon and the browser tab icon, and "1 Brunch a dois" becomes the Open Graph image so links shared on WhatsApp and Instagram show the café instead of the pancake alone.

### The rabbit hole (both falls)
- Replace the five PDF cut-outs with the six signature drinks. Each fall shows all six once, as now, in a different order.
- The drinks keep their wooden boards. Lit by the lamp and tilted a few degrees off the camera, a board with a shadow reads as a solid object, which a bare cup never did.
- Add the "Coffee Lovers" badge as a double-sided coin that turns as it drifts past, once per fall.
- Drop the pancake cut-out from the tunnel; it lives in the menu now.

### A Nossa História
Currently text and three playing cards. Add the wall-logo photograph in a storybook frame on the left, and turn the barista's shirt into the section's pull-quote, set in Fell italic with the photo beside it. The quote is Carroll's, it is on their staff's backs, and it is the best line the site has.

### A Experiência
- The main frame becomes the **magic lantern**: the ten campaign frames crossfade every 4 seconds with a slow zoom, inside the parchment frame, with a brass crank at the side. Turning the crank (drag) or clicking it advances a frame. It pauses when off-screen.
- The peeking second frame becomes "Sala" (checkerboard room).
- The polaroid strip gets the real photos: Pancakes (logo napkin), Brunch 2, Macha, Entrada, and keeps the two guest-with-rabbit shots.

### O Nosso Brunch & Café → the menu
- The four flip cards become a photographed menu with dish names from the media: Ovos Beneditinos, Salmon Benedict, Panquecas Rainha de Copas, Hambúrguer, Turcos, Salada de Camarão Gourmet, Bowl de Iogurte, Bagels. Front: name and suit. Back: the photo and one line.
- The Brunch strip drops the low-res lattes and pancake for Cappucino 3, Bowl yogurt and 2 OPorto Iced Latte.
- The menu overlay gains a second page, "Signature Coffee", listing the six drinks with their product shots. Prices are still missing for everything except the 25,50 € brunch.

### Signature Coffee (new chapter, between Brunch and Eventos)
The page's one purely indulgent section.
- **Backdrop**: "5 maquina Alice" or the "Fundo" table shot, darkened, with the parchment ground fading in over it.
- **Six drinks on a tea-party table**: the cut-outs stand in a row on a wooden shelf. Scroll or drag moves the shelf. The drink in the centre rises, tilts toward the cursor, and its name is written beneath in Fell with a two-line description: O'Porto Latte "com pastel de nata", Cappuccino Alice "pétalas de rosa e praliné", Alice Latte "com mini donut", Iced Alice Latte Caramelo, Iced Matcha Vanilla Strawberry, Iced Matcha Mango Vanilla.
- **Micro-interactions**: steam rises from the hot cups (small Canvas particles), ice clinks on the iced ones (a subtle vertical jitter), the straws are the hover targets.
- **CTA**: "Ver todos os cafés" opens the menu overlay on its coffee page.

### Eventos & Sala Privada
"Cave" replaces the PDF photo behind the door. "8 empregada das panquecas" joins as a second card peeking from the frame, since a private room needs a host in it.

### Localização & Horário
"Entrada" goes beside the map in a parchment frame with the caption "É esta a porta". The rabbit still runs to the pin, but now it runs to a door people can recognise from the street.

### O Que Dizem
"7 Esplanada pet friendly" sits behind the review that mentions pets, faded, so the words have proof.

### Contactos & footer
The round badge replaces the small rabbit in the footer, stamped, with a slow tilt on hover. It also becomes the preloader's watch face background at low opacity.

## 3. Processing pipeline

- **Cut-outs**: the three alpha PNGs are used as delivered. The three on white are keyed (white to alpha with edge matting), then all six exported at 1024 px WebP with alpha, about 60 to 90 KB each. The current five PDF cut-outs are deleted.
- **Photos**: everything resampled to 1600 px on the long side, WebP quality 80, with a JPEG fallback. Portrait photos also get a 4:5 crop for the polaroids.
- **Lantern frames**: 1400 px WebP quality 76, lazy-loaded when the section is within one screen, first frame preloaded. About 1.1 MB for the set, loaded once.
- **Badge**: 512 px WebP with alpha, plus a 64 px PNG favicon and a 180 px apple-touch-icon.
- **OG image**: "1 Brunch a dois" at 1200 × 630 JPEG with the wordmark overlaid bottom-left.

Budget after the change: roughly 2.2 MB more media on a full scroll, none of it above the fold, all lazy. Lighthouse stays above 90 on desktop; mobile drops the lantern to six frames.
