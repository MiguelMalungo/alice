Alice in Brewland

A one-page site where the scroll is the fall. Every section is a landing on the way down the rabbit hole; the space between sections is the descent itself, rendered in WebGL and driven by scroll.

What the Figma gives us

A Victorian storybook world: a watercolour White Rabbit, parchment scroll buttons, Queen-of-Hearts red, gold sparkles, a cream ground and a brown Victorian-script wordmark. The copy is playful and self-aware: “o tempo corre devagar, mas o café é rápido.” All of it stays. The site's job is to turn that world into something you move through.

The parchment CTAs are rebuilt as an SVG frame with live HTML text so they stay accessible, translatable and animatable. The illustrations are kept.

capítulo ii

The concept: the scroll is the fall

Alice doesn't walk into Wonderland; she falls. The page is one vertical descent. Sections are landings. Between them, a WebGL tunnel scrubs past the camera with drifting teacups, pocket watches, keys, playing cards and coffee beans. You land at the story, the tea party, the private room, the map, and at the very bottom the rabbit is waiting.

♥

A reason to move

Every animation is a beat in the descent, not a decoration on a static page.

♠

An arc

Beginning, middle and end. The motion gets darker and warmer as you go deeper, then resolves in the footer.

♦

One deliberate risk

The hero is not a photograph. It is the rabbit, the wordmark and a living hole in the ground. The first scroll drops you in.

Design system

The cream, brown and parchment palette belongs to the client and stays. What keeps it from feeling templated is the dark theme, where the descent turns the page into the inside of the hole, and a strict ration of red: one heart, in one place, per section. This page is set in the proposed system, so it doubles as a style tile.

Components

Parchment button

SVG scroll frame, live text, illustration slot. 3D tilt on hover, magnetic pull within 80 px, gold sparkle burst on click.

Playing card

Menu items and event types. Suit corner marks, Fell title, flips on hover or tap to reveal the tagline.

Storybook frame

Parchment-edged photo frame. A WebGL displacement ripple on hover: through the looking glass.

Pocket watch

SVG watch showing live Porto time and today's state: “Aberto até às 19h00” or “Abre amanhã às 8h30”. The hands really move.

Chapter rule

Hand-drawn line with the Queen's heart, redrawn as SVG so the line draws itself in and the heart pops.

Custom cursor

A ring that becomes a teacup over links, a watch over the hours, a “Bebe-me” tag over the menu. Off on touch.

capítulo v

Micro-interactions and motion rules

  • Masked line reveals for every heading and paragraph: clip per line, slide up, power3.out, 0.9 s, 0.06 s stagger.
  • Magnetic parchment buttons: lerp toward the cursor, spring back.
  • Sparkle burst on click: eight gold particles on Canvas 2D, 400 ms.
  • Heart chapter rule draws in with stroke-dashoffset.
  • Photo ripple on hover, decaying over 600 ms.
  • Card flip, rotateY 180° with backface hidden.
  • Cursor morph: ring to teacup, watch or tag.
  • Watch tick: minute hand steps, hour hand glides; live open state.
  • Rabbit ear parallax in the hero, ±12 px.
  • Smooth scroll with Lenis, lerp 0.09, synced to ScrollTrigger.

Rules

  • One easing family: power3 and expo. Reveals 0.6 to 1.2 s, hovers 0.25 s.
  • Nothing loops except the rabbit breathing and the tunnel drift.
  • Nothing moves without a reason tied to the story.
  • Reduced motion: the tunnel becomes a static gradient, reveals become fades, no parallax, native scroll.
  • Every section is fully readable at rest. Nothing is parked at opacity 0.
capítulo vi

Stack, budgets, fallbacks

  • Vite + TypeScript, vanilla DOM. A one-pager doesn't need a framework; GSAP integrates cleanest without one.
  • GSAP 3.13+, all plugins free: ScrollTrigger, SplitText, MotionPath, Draggable, Inertia, Flip.
  • Lenis for smooth scroll.
  • three.js for the hero hole and tunnels. One renderer, DPR capped at 1.5, Draco-compressed props, procedural walls with no textures.
  • Google Fonts with a preload on the display face.
  • AVIF/WebP with responsive srcset; the rabbit cut to 1600 px for web.
  • Vercel or Netlify static hosting; Plausible analytics, so no cookie banner.
  • Copy lives in content/pt.json, ready for an English toggle later.

Mobile and low-end

Under 768 px the tunnel becomes a lighter 2D canvas of falling objects, nothing pins, cards stack, the lazy susan becomes a swipe carousel, the cursor is off and tap replaces hover. With no WebGL or on a weak device the same layout runs with the 2D fallback.

Accessibility

All text is real HTML; WebGL is decorative and hidden from assistive tech. Every card, button and the carousel are keyboard-operable with a gold focus ring. Ink on cream holds 7:1; headings 4.5:1; red only on large text or with an icon.
